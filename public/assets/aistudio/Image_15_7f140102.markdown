## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, warm, and poetic digital presence tailored for luxury pret, festive formals, and unstitched artisanal womenswear. The aesthetic bridges timeless haute couture elegance with serene contemporary digital ergonomics. Drawing inspiration from classical atelier craft, sunlit linen canvases, architectural archways, and warm terracotta-chocolate leather, the visual language is poised, confident, and deeply feminine.

The design movement combines **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, uncluttered negative space evoking high-end coffee-table lookbooks.
- Crisp, authoritative serif typography paired with hyper-legible contemporary geometric sans-serif body copy.
- Warm monochromatic layering (deep espresso and chestnut grounded upon velvety off-white, light ecru, and petal-blush tints) in place of stark clinical whites or harsh grays.
- Delicate hairline borders, brushed bronze accents, and gentle ambient depth that celebrate craftsmanship and textile tactile sensibility.

## Colors

The palette embraces organic earthen warmth, rejecting sterile digital neutrals in favor of baked ceramic, rich cocoa, spun silk, and warm parchment.

- **Primary (`#5C3A21` — Deep Atelier Brown):** Used for primary headlines, focal CTAs, prominent logo marks, and structural framing. It imparts weight, classic prestige, and grounded warmth.
- **Secondary (`#C49A6C` — Muted Bronze Gold):** Serves as an accent for delicate hairline separators, badge borders, star ratings, metadata highlights, and hovered states.
- **Tertiary (`#F7E7E0` — Soft Petal Peach):** Applied selectively to subtle badge fills, active swatch borders, festive promo highlights, and subtle tonal hero banners.
- **Neutral Surface Base (`#FAF7F2` — Warm Cream / Ecru):** Forms the foundational background canvas. Paired with secondary surface `#F5EFEB` (Warm Alabaster) and border/divider tone `#EDE3DA` (Linen Sand). Text on neutral surfaces adopts deep cocoa (`#2E1B0E`) and rich brown (`#5C3A21`), retaining rich contrast without the severity of pure black `#000000`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the dramatic editorial high-contrast serif **Playfair Display** against the fluid, architectural clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Headings utilize Playfair Display. Editorial titles and hero banners benefit from generous tracking and disciplined line heights. Small decorative headlines and subheadings frequently leverage italicized serif expressions to highlight artisanal qualities.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Category headers, status chips, and ESTD brand notations use `label-lg`, `label-md`, or `label-sm` with full uppercase styling and expanded tracking (`0.12em` to `0.16em`), evoking luxury atelier stamps.
- **Body & Captions:** Product fabric descriptions, sizing charts, and checkout data rely on Plus Jakarta Sans, offering effortless scanability and neutral modernism that complements the decorative serif display.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is built on an editorial fluid grid with intentional, generous whitespace mimicking luxury fashion broadsheets.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** A 12-column layout governed by `margin: 4rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container is capped at 1440px to preserve photographic fidelity. Product listing views feature generous 3-column and 4-column card rhythms with staggered lookbook interruptions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** An 8-column layout with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Two-column product grids provide breathing room for intricate embroidery details and drape photography.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A 4-column layout governed by `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`. Sticky purchase bars, edge-to-edge lookbook carousels, and thumb-friendly touch targets (min 44px) ensure luxury remains effortlessly functional.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows deliberate vertical cadence: macro section intervals (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem-6rem in heroes) balance tight micro-details (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) within price, swatch, and badge clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface tinting and warm, diffused ambient shadows rather than sharp synthetic drop shadows.

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#FAF7F2`. Raised surfaces—such as modal drawers, floating size selectors, and navigation bars—adopt pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or warmed alabaster (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Elevated cards and popovers utilize low-opacity, warm chocolate-tinted drop shadows:
  - *Subtle Tier (Cards, hovering tiles):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Tier (Cart drawers, popups, sticky action bar):* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.06)`.
- **Delicate Hairline Borders:** Structural division relies on `1px solid #EDE3DA` (Linen Sand). Gold/bronze accent borders (`#C49A6C`) at 1px thickness are reserved for VIP membership badges, selected fabric variants, and exclusive seasonal capsules.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, prioritizing tailored, architectural corners over aggressive rounded bubbles to maintain high-fashion formality.

- Standard interactive controls (inputs, secondary buttons, notification banners) feature an understated `0.25rem` (4px) border radius.
- Cards, image picture containers, and modal sheets adopt `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Pills are strictly reserved for special interactive badges, unstitched suit component tags (e.g., "Dupatta Included", "Embroidered Lawn"), and floating CTA buttons where an elongated pill shape reinforces tactile luxury.
- Hero masks and editorial lookbook frames frequently introduce soft, subtle neoclassical arch curves on top borders (`32px` to `48px` curve radius) to reference classic atelier design.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Shop Now", "Add to Bag"):** Solid deep brown (`#5C3A21`) background with crisp off-white text (`#FAF7F2`), uppercase `label-lg` typography, `0.25rem` radius or refined soft pill, and 16px vertical by 32px horizontal padding. On hover, shifts softly to `#7A4E2D` with an ambient glow.
- **Secondary CTA:** Cream background (`#FAF7F2`) with 1px hairline border (`#5C3A21`), deep brown text, and subtle hover fill (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Underlined serif or tracked uppercase sans link with an animated hairline underline expanding from center.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background is crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or alabaster (`#F5EFEB`), encased in a 1px border of `#EDE3DA`. Focus states transition the border to `#5C3A21` with a faint `rgba(92, 58, 33, 0.08)` outer glow ring.
- Placeholder copy is rendered in muted warm taupe (`#A89689`). Floating labels follow `label-md` tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size variants and filter checkboxes feature 1px deep brown borders. Checked states display solid `#5C3A21` with a crisp white custom checkmark or inner bronze circular pip (`#C49A6C`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Collection Tags ("Pret", "Unstitched", "Festive"):** Rendered with `label-sm` uppercase, enclosed in `0.5rem` radius containers with a soft blush peach fill (`#F7E7E0`) and deep brown text (`#5C3A21`).
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped with `#EDE3DA` hairline borders, shifting to filled deep brown when selected.

### Product Cards
- Cards feature vertical 3:4 or 2:3 aspect ratio portrait photography. Frames use `0.5rem` corner radius, hairline `#EDE3DA` dividers, and no harsh outlines.
- Hover reveals secondary drape angles, a quick-view floating pill button, and a subtle warm lift shadow. Pricing is displayed in clean `body-md` bold, while collection names use `headline-sm`.

### Fabric & Color Swatches
- Color and print selectors are circular (28px diameter), spaced with `space-sm`. Active selection is demarcated by a 1px concentric gap and a 1px deep brown or bronze outer framing ring.

### Value Proposition Ribbon
- A streamlined horizontal trust bar featuring delicate linear icons (e.g., Trusted Brand, Premium Quality, Fast Delivery, 24/7 Concierge) separated by 1px `#EDE3DA` vertical dividers, set in `label-md` uppercase typography against a warm ecru surface.
